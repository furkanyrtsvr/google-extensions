About & Help

Smart Fix Feature

Select any text containing a number and unit (e.g., "500 mA") to instantly convert or format it.

Word Online / Office 365

Due to browser security in Word Online, the extension uses "Clipboard Mode". Click "Fix/Convert" to copy the result, then press Ctrl+V to paste.

Google Docs Compatibility

To enable text selection detection in Google Docs, you must enable Screen Reader Support:
Tools > Accessibility > Turn on screen reader support.

🔒 Privacy & Security

This extension runs 100% locally on your device. No text or data is ever sent to external servers. It is safe to use with sensitive documents.
